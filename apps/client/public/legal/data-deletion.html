User Data Deletion

Sài Gòn, a little journey · Last updated 1 November 2026

You can remove everything Sài Gòn, a little journey stores about you. The fastest way is right inside the game; you can also ask us to delete it for you. Both options remove your guest profile, journey progress, and the conversation memories neighbors keep. For a reminder of what we store, see the Privacy Policy.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

Clear saved data

  1. Start the game and open the menu (the Journal / pause panel).
  2. Choose Clear saved data.

This removes the session token from this browser and detaches this device from your save, so nothing identifies you here anymore.

Start a new journey

Choose Start a new journey from the same menu to wipe your current progress and conversation memories and begin fresh.

If you signed in with Meta (Facebook)

You can disconnect the game from your Meta account at any time from your Facebook settings, under Apps and Websites. Removing the app there revokes our access. To also delete the save tied to that account, send a deletion request below and include the account you used.

Request deletion by email

Prefer us to handle it, or lost access to your save? Email privacy@saigon-journey.example with the subject Data deletion request. Include your in-game name (and, if you used Meta sign-in, the account) so we can find the right save.

  • We confirm the request and delete your data within 30 days.
  • Deletion removes your guest profile, progress, completed errands, and conversation memories. It cannot be undone.
