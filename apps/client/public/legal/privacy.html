Privacy Policy

Sài Gòn, a little journey · Last updated 1 November 2026

Sài Gòn, a little journey (“the game”, “we”) is a browser game where you wander a small slice of the city, meet neighbors, and carry your progress between visits. This policy explains what we store so that experience works, and how you can remove it at any time. We keep the data we collect to the minimum the game needs to run.

What we store

  • Your guest profile: a randomly generated player id, the display name you choose, and a character color.
  • Your journey: in-game progress such as quests, completed errands, reputation, currency, and your last position in the city.
  • Conversation memories: short notes neighbors (AI characters) remember about your visits, so a chat can pick up where it left off. These are game-world facts, not transcripts of everything you type.
  • A session token: stored in your browser so the game can recognize your save the next time you return.
  • If you sign in with Meta (Facebook Login): we receive a stable account identifier used only to link your save to your account. We do not post anything, read your friends, or import your profile details beyond what is needed to identify the account.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, and no selling of your data.
  • No analytics profiles built to follow you across other sites.
  • No posting to your social accounts.

How we use it

The data above exists only to run the game: to load your save, keep multiplayer presence in sync, let neighbors respond in context, and restore your journey when you come back.

AI conversations

Neighbors in the game are powered by AI models. Messages you send to a neighbor are processed to generate a reply and may produce a few short “memory” notes as described above. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in these chats.

Where it lives and how long

Saves are stored on our game server for as long as your journey is active. Inactive guest saves are periodically cleared. You can remove your data yourself at any time from inside the game, or request deletion — see the Data Deletion page.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email privacy@saigon-journey.example.
