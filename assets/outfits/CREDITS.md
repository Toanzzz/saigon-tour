# Wardrobe outfits

Avatar meshes, skeletons and animations derive from Kenney Mini Characters
(CC0), preserved in `../mini-characters/` with its original `License.txt`.

Garment meshes and material assignments are project-authored. Both body variants
retain the original player rig. Editable sources are the `.blend` files;
`scripts/blender/build-outfits.py` rebuilds the source `.glb` files.

The festival áo dài uses midnight-blue cloth, gold embroidered motifs, and the
same body-specific rig and split tunic panels.

Runtime copies are Draco-compressed by `scripts/prepare-wardrobe-assets.ts`.
